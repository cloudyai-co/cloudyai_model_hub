What's an AIBOM?

An AI Bill of Materials is a structured inventory of what goes into a model — architecture, training data, licenses, evaluation results, known limitations. It's the AI-supply-chain equivalent of an SBOM: the same idea, applied to models instead of packages, so security, compliance, and provenance questions have a real answer instead of a shrug.
